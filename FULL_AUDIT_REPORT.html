STEP 1 — Executive Summary

Gilaf Store is a custom PHP ecommerce platform selling premium Kashmiri food products. It has a strong SEO foundation (dedicated 782-line SEO library, JSON-LD, OG tags) and good trust signals (FSSAI, lab tested, organic badges). However, 4 critical issues undermine performance and search visibility.

What’s Working Well

  • Dynamic <title>, meta description, canonical with intelligent fallbacks
  • Multi-variant Product JSON-LD with real AggregateRating (no fake stars)
  • Complete Open Graph + Twitter Card coverage (product-specific OG tags)
  • Organization + WebSite schema on all pages with SearchAction
  • Self-hosted fonts (no Google Fonts CDN — faster)
  • Chatbot lazy-loaded on interaction (good performance pattern)
  • Full reviews system, trust badges, FSSAI, batch verification
  • 7 shipping partner logos, 6 policy pages, support ticket system

Critical Issues Found

  • www vs non-www domain split: Canonicals use www.gilafstore.com, all sitemaps use gilafstore.com
  • Logo <h1> on every page: Every page has 2+ <h1> tags
  • 30 product images >200 KB: 12 images are >5 MB each (total ~103 MB)
  • 109 KB unminified CSS on every page: mobile-homepage-fix.css loaded everywhere
